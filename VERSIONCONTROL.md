## Version Control

<div align="center">
  
```X.Y.Z.u```
</div>

Each version code follows a standardized nomenclature that clarifies the magnitude of the modifications made. In the table below, each letter in the version code is broken down into a separate column, explaining its meaning and significance.

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Version Component</th>
      <th>Type of Modification</th>
      <th>Description</th>
      <th>Example</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>X</strong></td>
      <td>Major</td>
      <td>Significant changes that may alter core functionality or introduce major new features.</td>
      <td><code>2.0.0.u</code> – A completely new version with big updates!</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Y</strong></td>
      <td>Medium</td>
      <td>Notable improvements, feature additions, or performance enhancements without a complete overhaul.</td>
      <td><code>2.1.0.u</code> – New functionalities and important optimizations!</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Z</strong></td>
      <td>Minor</td>
      <td>Small refinements, fixes, and internal adjustments to enhance usability.</td>
      <td><code>2.1.5.u</code> – Minor improvements and bug fixes!</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>u</strong></td>
      <td>Urgent</td>
      <td>Critical updates addressing security vulnerabilities or severe bugs that need immediate attention.</td>
      <td><code>2.1.5.1</code> – Emergency patch to resolve a major issue!</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
